## 2. Deal types, ranked by how fast they close

| Rank | Deal type | Typical size | Close time | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Lease rental discounting (LRD)** on Mumbai commercial property (BKC, Lower Parel, Andheri E, Powai, Thane) | ₹50–300 cr | 4–6 weeks | Leased-property collateral, simple underwriting, many lenders compete |
| 2 | **Refinancing expensive debt** (swap 13–16% NBFC/private debt for cheaper) | ₹50–200 cr | 4–8 weeks | Borrower is motivated, the savings pay your fee |
| 3 | **Construction and last-mile funding for Mumbai redevelopment** (society redevelopment, SRA, cluster schemes) | ₹75–300 cr | 5–8 weeks with private credit | Huge demand, private credit funds very active |
| 4 | Promoter / acquisition / special-situations finance | ₹100–500 cr | 6–10 weeks | Biggest fees (2–3%) but slower. Take only 1 |
| 5 | Mid-corporate capex (renewables, data centres, warehousing, EV components, pharma, specialty chemicals) | ₹100–300 cr | 8–12 weeks | Mostly lands in January; start now for Q1 2027 |

**Rule:** fill the pipeline mostly with types 1–3. They are the only ones that reliably pay out before Christmas.

## 3. Lenders (register with 12–15 of them in the first 2 weeks)

- **Private credit funds** (performing-credit and special-situations funds): fastest decisions, pay arrangers.
- **NBFCs / housing finance companies** with real-estate and mid-corporate books.
- **Private banks' mid-corporate and LRD desks.**
- **Foreign banks** (for good-quality borrowers and LRD).
- **Family offices** (bridge loans, last-mile funding).

---

## 4. Where the clients are (all from public or referral sources)

1. **CA firms in Mumbai.** Sign 10–15 as referral partners and pay them 10–15% of your fee. Best source of warm introductions.
2. **Records of existing loans (MCA charge index):** shows who borrowed, from whom, how much, and when. If a charge was created 2–3 years ago with an NBFC, that company is a refinance candidate.
3. **Credit-rating press releases:** rated loan amounts, upgrades (can refinance cheaper) and downgrades (need special-situations money).
4. **Redevelopment consultants, architects and project management firms,** plus developer associations. They know every society that is stuck on funding.
5. **Commercial property brokers:** owners of fully leased buildings are the prospects for type-1 deals.
6. **SME IPO pipeline:** companies that need pre-IPO funding or restructuring.

## 7. Fee terms (non-negotiable in the mandate letter)

- Retainer on signing: ₹10–25 L, **non-refundable**, adjusted against the success fee.
- Success fee: 1.5–2% of the amount paid out (2–3% for special situations), **payable on payout, not on approval**.
- Exclusivity: 90 days. Tail clause: fee is due if a lender you introduced funds within 12 months.
- Fee deducted directly from the loan payout (escrow instruction or borrower's letter to the lender). This is the single biggest protection for collections.
