Read your numbers straight from X

A bookmark that reads the views, likes, replies, reposts and bookmarks shown under your posts, plus your follower count, while you're signed in to X in your own browser. It only reads what's on screen. Nothing is posted, and your X password never leaves your browser.

On a computer (Chrome, Safari, Edge)

  1. Show your bookmarks bar, then drag this button onto it: X numbers
  2. Open x.com/Sudhirkrs17 (your profile, Posts tab), signed in.
  3. Click the X numbers bookmark. Wait about 20 seconds while it scrolls.
  4. Click Review and save in Control Panel, check the numbers, then Save.

On iPhone or Android

  1. Tap Copy bookmark code:
  2. Bookmark any page in Safari or Chrome, then edit that bookmark: name it X numbers and paste the code as its address.
  3. Open x.com/Sudhirkrs17 in that browser (not the X app), signed in. Then open the bookmark: in Safari from the bookmarks list; in Chrome by typing "X numbers" in the address bar and tapping the bookmark suggestion.

Run it weekly, before reading Insights. If X changes its page design and the bookmark finds no numbers, log them by hand under Log numbers.
