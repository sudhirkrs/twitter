# ₹5 Crore by 30 Dec 2026: Roadmap, Worked Backwards (Mumbai)

> Start date: 27 Sep 2026 · Finish line: 30 Dec 2026 · **94 days**

---

## 0. The math first

| | |
|---|---|
| Target | ₹5,00,00,000 |
| Days left | 94 |
| Needed per day | **₹5.32 lakh, every day, Sundays included** |
| Needed per week | **~₹37 lakh** |
| If "₹5 cr" means *in hand after tax* | ~₹7.8 cr of taxable profit (30% slab + 15% surcharge + 4% cess ≈ 35.9%) |

What the math rules out:

- **A salary, a job switch or normal freelancing can't get there.** At ₹5 lakh a day you are making deals, not billing hours.
- **Only four things can move ₹5 cr in 94 days:**
  1. **Selling or monetising an asset you already own** (property, land, business stake, ESOPs, IP).
  2. **Commission on very large transactions** (real estate, business sale, fundraising or procurement advisory).
  3. **High-ticket B2B contracts** (₹25 lakh to ₹1 cr each, several of them).
  4. **Leveraged speculation (F&O, crypto, "operator" tips).** ❌ This is excluded. SEBI's own studies found that about 9 out of 10 individual F&O traders lose money. "No excuses" means no gambling either.

So the plan runs on engines 1–3.

## 1. Picture 30 Dec 2026 and read the ledger backwards

On 30 Dec your bank statement shows ₹5 cr. Here is one realistic way those credits got there:

| Date received | Source | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| 27 Dec | Final milestone, enterprise contract #3 | ₹60 L |
| 20 Dec | Commission, commercial/luxury property deal #2 | ₹1.2 cr |
| 12 Dec | Advance + milestone, enterprise contracts #2 & #4 | ₹90 L |
| 25 Nov | Commission, property deal #1 (booked in the Diwali muhurat window) | ₹1.0 cr |
| 10 Nov | Enterprise contract #1 (50% advance) | ₹50 L |
| Oct–Dec | Asset monetisation / smaller deals / retainers | ₹80 L |
| | **Total** | **₹5.0 cr** |

Working backwards from that ledger:

- Money **collected** by late December needs contracts **signed** by late November.
- Contracts signed in November need **meetings in October**.
- Meetings in October need a **list of 150–300 qualified targets by 5 Oct**.
- Rule of thumb: you need **3–5× pipeline coverage**, so **₹15–25 cr of live, qualified pipeline by 15 Oct**.

## 2. Choose your engine based on what you have today

| What you have on 27 Sep | Primary engine | What ₹5 cr needs |
|---|---|---|
| Property, land, business stake, ESOPs | **Monetise or restructure the asset** | A sale, a partial stake sale, or a lease-plus-deposit deal. This is the most likely route. |
| Network of HNIs, founders, CXOs; no capital | **Transaction advisory / brokerage** (Mumbai real estate, business sales, fund-raise) | At 1–2% commission, about ₹250–500 cr of deal value (or a share of it via co-broking). Mumbai has ₹30–100 cr ticket deals in Worli, BKC, Lower Parel, Juhu and Bandra. MahaRERA agent registration is mandatory for real estate. |
| Sellable skill (AI/automation, tech, sales, compliance, design) | **High-ticket B2B contracts** | 5–10 contracts at ₹50 L–₹1 cr, or 20 at ₹25 L. Sell fixed-price outcomes ("cut your ops cost by ₹X cr"), not hours. |
| An existing audience (X/Twitter, YouTube, LinkedIn, community) | **Productise the audience** | 1,000 buyers × ₹50,000, or 10,000 × ₹5,000, plus sponsorships. This only works if the audience already exists; you can't build one from zero in 94 days. |
| ₹50 L+ of your own capital | **Fast-turnover trade or deal financing** with an operator you know, in a business you understand | Only with capital you can afford to lose. No borrowed money at high interest. |

**Best odds: stack two engines.** Use one big-ticket engine for the ₹3–4 cr and a faster-cash engine for the ₹1–2 cr.

## 3. The 94-day calendar

### Phase 1: Set up and build the target list (28 Sep – 12 Oct · 15 days)
- [ ] Decide your engine(s) by **30 Sep**. Write a one-line offer for each: *who pays, for what outcome, at what price*.
- [ ] Get the legal basics in place: GST registration, a current account, MahaRERA agent registration if you're doing real estate, and templates for NDA, mandate letter and service contract.
- [ ] Build a **target list of 300 names** (decision-makers, not companies) in a spreadsheet: name, role, why now, warm intro path.
- [ ] Credibility kit: a one-page PDF, 3 case studies or proof points, and a LinkedIn/X profile rewritten around the offer.
- [ ] Outreach starts **1 Oct**: 40 touches a day (calls, WhatsApp, email, intros).
- **Checkpoint 12 Oct:** 25+ meetings booked and ₹10 cr+ pipeline identified.

### Phase 2: Close the first money and use the festive window (13 Oct – 15 Nov · 34 days)
- [ ] Run **3 meetings a day**. Every meeting ends with a next step that has a date.
- [ ] **Dussehra → Dhanteras → Diwali (early–mid Nov)** is peak season for Mumbai property bookings and corporate gifting and spending decisions. Push closings into this window.
- [ ] Get your first contract signed with an **advance of at least 30–50%**. Cash in the bank beats a signed PO.
- [ ] Recycle your first win: turn it into a case study and ask the client for 3 referrals.
- **Checkpoint 31 Oct:** first ₹25 L collected and ₹20 cr qualified pipeline. **Checkpoint 15 Nov:** ₹1.5 cr collected or contracted.

### Phase 3: Scale and close the big tickets (16 Nov – 15 Dec · 30 days)
- [ ] Put 80% of your time on the **top 10 deals by value**. Drop anything under ₹10 L unless it closes this week.
- [ ] Bring in partners on revenue share (co-brokers, delivery subcontractors, a closer). Giving up 30% of a ₹1 cr deal beats 100% of nothing.
- [ ] **15 Dec: pay the advance-tax instalment** (75% of estimated tax due). Missing it adds interest.
- **Checkpoint 15 Dec:** ₹3.5 cr collected, ₹1.5 cr+ invoiced with firm payment dates.

### Phase 4: Collect everything (16 Dec – 30 Dec · 15 days)
- [ ] Only collected money counts. Chase every invoice daily, politely and in writing.
- [ ] Offer a 2–3% early-payment discount to anyone who pays before 24 Dec. Many offices slow down between Christmas and New Year.
- [ ] 30 Dec: reconcile the bank statement. That number is your score.

## 5. Kill-switch rules (so you don't waste 94 days on one bet)

- **12 Oct:** if pipeline is under ₹5 cr, your offer or target list is wrong. Change the offer, not the effort level.
- **31 Oct:** if nothing has been collected, add the second engine immediately (asset monetisation or a partnership).
- **30 Nov:** if you're under ₹2 cr collected, go all-in on the 3 largest deals and bring in a senior closer or partner on revenue share.

---

## 6. Hard lines (breaking these costs you more than missing the target)

- ❌ No F&O, crypto leverage or "tips" to catch up.
- ❌ No borrowing (personal loans, credit cards, informal lenders) to fund the attempt.
- ❌ No "guaranteed return" schemes, MLM or unregistered investment pools. Mumbai has plenty, and they target people in a hurry.
- ❌ No cash receipts of ₹2 lakh or more (Sec 269ST penalty = 100% of the amount). Everything goes through banking channels and is invoiced.
- ✅ Keep 36% of every rupee of profit aside for tax the day it lands.

---

## 7. The honest note

₹5 cr in 94 days is achievable for someone who already has an asset, a network, or a sellable high-ticket skill. It is not achievable from zero through effort alone. If 30 Dec lands at ₹1.5–2 cr, the pipeline, partners and credibility you built can take you to ₹5 cr+ in the first half of 2027. That isn't a failure; the business keeps compounding after the deadline.
