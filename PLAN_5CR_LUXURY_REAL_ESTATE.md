# ₹5 Cr Profit by 30 Dec 2026: Mumbai Luxury Real Estate and Office Leasing

Vertical: **transaction advisory for wealthy buyers (luxury homes) and companies (office space)**.
Edge: access to wealthy people and senior executives, credibility on financial questions, and a finance-first way of pitching property ("yield, price per sq ft, how easily you can sell it later", not "sea view").
Vehicle: Private Limited company, registered as a real estate agent with MahaRERA.

## 2. When the money arrives (the constraint that sets every date)

Developers usually pay the agent's commission **after the buyer has paid 10–20% and the sale agreement is registered**, which is often 30–60 days after booking.

```
Commission in bank by 20–28 Dec
  ← sale agreement registered by ~5–10 Dec
  ← buyer pays 10–20% by ~25 Nov
  ← BOOKING by 15 Nov (Diwali window: Dhanteras 6 Nov, Diwali 8 Nov)
  ← site visit + negotiation by 1 Nov
  ← first conversation by 20 Oct
```

**So 8 of the 10 bookings must happen by 15 Nov.** Bookings after 1 Dec will mostly pay out in January.

## 3. Funnel

```
300 wealthy contacts → 80 conversations → 40 site visits → 15 negotiations → 10 bookings
(listed by 4 Oct)      (by 25 Oct)        (by 8 Nov)        (by 12 Nov)       (8 by 15 Nov, 2 by 5 Dec)
```

Who buys a ₹10–60 cr home in Q4:
- Company owners, senior executives, partners at CA and law firms, doctors running hospitals
- **Founders and employees who just cashed out** (startup share sales, recent IPOs)
- **NRIs from Dubai, Singapore, London and the US.** Their peak India visit and buying season is **December**, so they are the target for the last 2 bookings.
- Family offices buying **several units at once** at a discount (developers pay a higher commission on these)

---

## 4. Supply side: sign up with 6–8 projects, not 60

- Pick **6–8 luxury projects** (ready to move in or nearly finished sell fastest: less risk for the buyer, faster registration).
- Negotiate **written commission terms**: rate, *payment within 15 days of agreement registration*, and a bonus for bulk buys.
- Ask for a "**private preview**" allocation: pre-approved discounts or better floors/units for your clients. That is the reason they'd buy through you.
- Developers fund the events: sample-flat evenings for 8–12 guests.

---

## 5. Office leasing (the second engine)

- Target **5–8 companies** from your network that are expanding, especially global companies setting up offices in India (BKC, Powai, Thane, Navi Mumbai, Andheri East).
- Represent the tenant: shortlist → compare 3 buildings → negotiate rent, rent-free months and fit-out budget → landlord pays you.
- Big property firms dominate this market. Co-broke with one of them where needed (a 50% share of a ₹3 cr fee is still ₹1.5 cr).
- Leasing takes 8–12 weeks, so **start 1 Oct**. Only 1 deal needs to close by December.

## 9. Rules

- MahaRERA registration **before** any deal is earned; commissions in the company's name, by bank transfer, with GST invoices.
- Never take a cash component of a property deal ("black" part of the price). It's illegal, and one such deal can end the business.
- Tell every buyer in writing who is paying you. Your reputation with wealthy buyers is the whole asset.
